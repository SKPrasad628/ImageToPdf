File requirements & privacy

Your files are processed in this browser. Page Forge does not upload your images or PDFs.

  • JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP: up to 256 MiB and 256 megapixels (65,535 pixels per side). Large images are automatically fitted to at most 16 megapixels and 8,192 pixels per side, preserving aspect ratio.
  • Fitted images use lossless PNG working copies. Their dimensions may be reduced further to fit the available working-file budget. Your device originals stay unchanged.
  • PDFs: up to 50 MiB. A document may contain up to 200 pages.
  • Animated images become a still picture. TIFF, SVG, and unverifiable image formats need conversion first.
  • Working files and history share a 128 MiB budget. Raster exports are limited to 64 megapixels in total and 128 MiB of output.

About Page Forge

A quiet place to gather photos and PDF pages, arrange a document, and check the paper before you forge it.

Page Forge runs in your browser. Its blue-and-gold identity uses original geometric ornament inspired by ancient archives and celestial instruments.

Paper size, margins, placement, and quality stay in your hands. Untouched PDF pages can retain their text and vectors; the binding options explain when image editing or document changes affect preservation.

File requirements & privacy

Your selected images, PDFs, and generated output are processed locally in this browser. Page Forge does not send them to an upload service.

Layout preferences and appearance are saved in browser storage. Open documents and edit history stay in the current tab; closing or reloading it discards unfinished work. Your downloaded PDF remains wherever your browser saves downloads.

Supported files and limits
  • JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP: up to 256 MiB, 256 megapixels, and 65,535 pixels per side. Animated images become a still picture.
  • Large images are fitted to lossless working copies of at most 16 megapixels and 8,192 pixels per side, or smaller to fit the available budget. Original files on your device stay unchanged.
  • PDFs: up to 50 MiB. Documents: up to 200 pages.
  • Working files and history share a 128 MiB budget. Raster exports are limited to 64 megapixels in total and 128 MiB of output.
  • TIFF, SVG, and unverifiable formats need conversion first.

The fonts and PDF libraries are served with the app. A hosting provider may keep ordinary page-request logs; those requests do not contain your selected files.

How to use Page Forge

  1. Add files: images and PDFs share one document. Upload order becomes page order. To guarantee your exact selection sequence, add files one at a time.
  2. Arrange: drag pages, or choose Move earlier/later in Page actions. Use Select pages and the checkboxes for bulk changes.
  3. Edit: choose a page, then Edit page to rotate, flip, crop, resize, or adjust its image. Update preview prepares a tool change; Save this page commits the edits.
  4. Layout: choose Change layout for paper, margins, placement, image compression, and PDF preservation. Phones have Pages, Preview, and Layout views.
  5. Forge PDF: download your document and check the result for actual size or any omitted pages.
