File requirements & privacy

Your files are processed in this browser. Page Forge does not upload your images or PDFs.

  • JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and BMP: up to 32 MiB, 16 megapixels, and 8,192 pixels per side.
  • PDFs: up to 50 MiB. A document may contain up to 200 pages.
  • Animated images become a still picture. TIFF, SVG, and unverifiable image formats need conversion first.
  • Working files and history share a 128 MiB budget. Raster exports are limited to 64 megapixels in total and 128 MiB of output.

The forgotten archive

About Page Forge

A quiet place to gather photos and PDF pages, arrange a document, and check the paper before you forge it.

Page Forge runs in your browser. Its blue-and-gold identity uses original geometric ornament inspired by ancient archives and celestial instruments.

Paper size, margins, placement, and quality stay in your hands. Untouched PDF pages can retain their text and vectors; the binding options explain when image editing or document changes affect preservation.

Your work stays yours

Privacy, plainly

Your selected images, PDFs, and generated output are processed locally in this browser. Page Forge does not send them to an upload service.

Layout preferences and appearance are saved in browser storage. Open documents and edit history stay in the current tab; closing or reloading it discards unfinished work. Your downloaded PDF remains wherever your browser saves downloads.

The fonts and PDF libraries are served with the app. A hosting provider may keep ordinary page-request logs; those requests do not contain your selected files.

Tools of the archive

Shape every page

  • Arrange: drag pages, or use Earlier and Later beside the preview.
  • Edit: rotate, crop, resize, flip, and adjust images in the page editor.
  • Combine: add images and PDFs to the same document.
  • Bind: set paper size, margins, image placement, and quality.
